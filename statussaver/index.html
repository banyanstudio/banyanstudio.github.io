Private by design

No account, no ads, and your photos and videos never leave your device. The app reads only media already on your phone through Android’s scoped storage — no “all files access” permission. Anonymous usage and crash reporting can be switched off in Settings.

Statuses appear in the app once you’ve viewed them in WhatsApp itself — that’s when the media is downloaded to your phone.
