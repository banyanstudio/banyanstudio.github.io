1. What the App does

Nodi is a personal attendance tracker. You check in when your workday starts and check out when it ends; the App records those times and marks the day Present or Half day from how much of your configured workday had passed. You can also mark any day yourself as Present, Half day, Absent or Holiday, correct punch times, view a month calendar and summary, view weekly and yearly patterns, use a home-screen widget, receive a reminder before your workday ends, and export or import your records as a backup file.

The App works entirely on your device. It has no internet access and is not connected to any employer, payroll, HR or time-and-attendance system.

2. A personal record, not an official one

  • Recorded times come from your device's clock. Changing the clock or time zone, or entering or correcting times by hand, changes what is recorded.
  • The Present / Half day status and the summary figures are calculated by the App's own simple rules from the settings you choose. They will not necessarily match your employer's policies, your contract, or any law that applies to you.

Do not rely on the App as your only evidence of hours worked, attendance, leave or pay. If those matter to you, keep the records your employer or the law requires.
