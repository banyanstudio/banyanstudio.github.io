Every day you check in lands on the calendar, coloured by how it went.

One tap in, one tap out

Check in when you start and out when you leave. Check out before half your workday is up and the day is marked Half day; otherwise it's Present. Forgot to punch? Fix the time later from the calendar.

Your month at a glance

A calendar coloured by Present, Half day, Absent and Holiday, with a running count and your average week. Patterns shows when you worked each day of the week, and the whole year on one screen.

Widget and reminder

Check in or out straight from your home screen. And a reminder before your workday ends, so you remember to check out.

Stays on your phone

Nodi has no internet access at all — no account, no ads, no analytics. Export a backup file whenever you like, and import it on a new phone.

Nodi is for keeping your own record. It isn’t connected to your employer’s systems and isn’t an official timesheet.
